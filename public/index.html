About me

A little less formal

👋 Welcome! I am an Assistant Professor (tenure-track) at the Department of Computer Science, University of Bari Aldo Moro, Italy, where I conduct research in deep learning and computer vision. My work focuses on intelligent systems that can learn, perceive and reason from data, with applications spanning e-health, drone vision and digital humanities, among other domains.

If you happen to visit the department, you'll usually find me either in my sixth-floor office or, most often, in the CILAB lab on the fourth floor, where the real magic happens. Every now and then, I also make a strategic stop in Professor Giovanna Castellano's office, mainly to “borrow” a few taralli and share them with whoever happens to be around!

Students

Theses & supervision

Students with a strong interest in AI, solid programming skills, and motivation for research are welcome to work on their thesis under my supervision on one of our ongoing research topics. Whenever possible, thesis projects may also be carried out in collaboration with local companies, giving students the opportunity to combine academic work with hands-on experience through external internships.
