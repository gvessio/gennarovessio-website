I enjoy the community side of research: reviewing, organising events, building small communities around emerging topics, and occasionally discovering that organising a workshop requires almost as much debugging as writing code.

## Leadership and community roles

I am the **Chair of IAPR Technical Committee 19 — Computer Vision for Cultural Heritage Applications**, serving for the 2024–2026 and 2026–2028 terms.

In August 2026, I received an **IAPR Distinction Award**, together with Vice Chair Guillaume Caron, in recognition of our strategic leadership of TC19.

## Reviewing and Program Committees

I regularly serve as a reviewer and Program Committee member for international conferences in artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision and pattern recognition.

In 2026 I served as **Area Chair for NeurIPS**.

Selected conferences include **AAAI, CVPR, ECCV, ACL, ECML PKDD, ECAI, WACV, ICPR, CBMS, IJCNN, ICDAR** and **IEEE VIS**.

I also regularly review for journals including **Nature, International Journal of Computer Vision, Pattern Recognition, Remote Sensing of Environment, IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics, IEEE Transactions on Biometrics, Behavior, and Identity Science, Artificial Intelligence in Medicine, Expert Systems with Applications, Information Sciences, Knowledge-Based Systems, Machine Learning, Heritage Science, Journal on Computing and Cultural Heritage**, and **International Journal on Document Analysis and Recognition**.

In 2025 I was recognised as a **Distinguished Program Committee Member at ECML PKDD** for my contribution to the Research Track.

## Outreach

I regularly take part in public-engagement and science-communication activities on artificial intelligence and its applications, including seminars, public events, school initiatives and interviews.

In 2025 I published the popular-science book **_Intelligenza artificiale per curiosi: una mini-guida scientifica per tutti_** with Edizioni Dedalo.

Over the years I have contributed to events and initiatives including *Il Libro Possibile*, *Art & Science across Italy*, *FameLab* and *La Notte della Ricerca*, and have given specialist lectures for professional associations, companies and public institutions.

I have also taught several editions of training activities on **graphometric signature analysis**, organised by the Italian Graphological Association and its local partners. This long-running collaboration grew out of my earlier research on explainable decision-support tools for forensic handwriting examination.
