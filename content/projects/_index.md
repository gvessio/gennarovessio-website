I like research projects when they create the conditions to test ideas outside a benchmark and, ideally, to build something that somebody can actually use.

## Projects I lead or have led

### VISTA — Visual Assistance from Drones for Roadside Rescue

**2024–2026 · Principal Investigator · Italian Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport · €200k**

VISTA investigates the use of drones, computer vision and AI to improve situational awareness and support post-crash emergency response. The project combines real-world experimentation with local authorities and emergency stakeholders with research on visual assistance from autonomous aerial platforms.

### ACCESSO — Cash-Flow Management

**2026–2027 · Scientific lead of the University research contract · €111.6k**

Research and development of the Cash-Flow Management component within the ACCESSO project, funded under the PIA / PR Puglia FESR-FSE+ 2021–2027 framework.

### ISCRA C / CINECA

**2023–present · Principal Investigator**

A sequence of competitive high-performance computing projects supporting our research:

- **SPARTAN** — Sparse Autoencoder-based Representation Tuning for Accurate Networks (2026–2027)
- **ARTURO** — Advancing Multimodal and Efficient Computer Vision for Autonomous Drones (2025–2026)
- **MOSES** — Multimodal Models for Semantic Image Understanding (2024–2025)
- **DIANA** — Deep Learning for Artwork Inpainting and Captioning (2023–2024)

### PON AIM — Computational Methods for Cultural Heritage

**2019–2023 · PON Attraction and International Mobility**

My RTD-A position was funded through this programme, allowing me to develop a research line at the intersection of AI and cultural heritage that shaped much of my subsequent work on computer vision and visual arts.

## Projects I contribute to


### AMICA — Intelligent Assistance for Active Ageing

**2026–2027 · Italian Ministry of Health · FSC 2014–2020**

Research on AI-based assistance for active and healthy ageing in indoor and outdoor environments, with a focus on technologies supporting older adults' autonomy and well-being.

### bioTIMBRE

**2024–2027 · Spanish Ministry of Science, Innovation and Universities**

Research on robust hand-movement biometrics through computational and robotic methods, coordinated by the Universidad de Las Palmas de Gran Canaria.

### EDIH4DT

**2024–2026 · Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy · PNRR**

Digital transformation activities for public administrations and companies within the European Digital Innovation Hubs initiative.

### SAFETY

**2024–2026 · Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy · PNRR**

AI and data-driven methods for personalised healthy-food pathways against obesity, funded through the Accordi per l'Innovazione programme.

## Earlier projects

Previous activities include **NEED — Next-Gen Digital Diagnostics**, a PNC-funded project on explainable and trustworthy AI for digital diagnostics; and the PNRR-funded projects **MIAFF — Modello Integrato di Analisi dei Fabbisogni Formativi**, on training-needs analysis for small local authorities; **FAIR — Future Artificial Intelligence Research**, where I contributed to Spoke 6 on Symbiotic AI; and **GRINS — Growing Resilient, INclusive and Sustainable**, where I contributed to research on territorial sustainability.

Earlier work also includes the **HAND** PRIN project on handwriting analysis against neuromuscular diseases and a multidisciplinary **Horizon Europe Seeds** project on the digitisation and valorisation of written cultural heritage.
